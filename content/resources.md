- [Introduction to Library Research](https://libguides.uidaho.edu/Engl102libraryunit) - guide.
- [Library Data Hub](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/datahub/) - Data Hub Help Desk provides specialized support in the areas of artificial intelligence, geographic information systems, data sciences, data analysis and visualization, and research data management. 
    - [AI Basics Guide](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/datahub/ai-basics.html)
    - [Citing and Disclosing AI](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/datahub/citing-ai.html)
- [Vandal Chat](https://chat.uidaho.edu/) -  AI chat assistant powered by MindRouter. It is great for drafting and editing writing, explaining concepts, summarizing and asking questions about your documents, working with data, writing code, brainstorming, and translating. [Help page](https://chat.uidaho.edu/help/01_getting-started).
- [MindRouter](https://mindrouter.uidaho.edu/) - the University of Idaho’s own generative AI service. Anyone with a Vandal account can sign in. It runs on University hardware and is managed by Research Computing and Data Services, so no data is sent to non-campus entities.
- [RCDS Tutorials](https://hpc.uidaho.edu/compute/Tutorials/)
- [AI at U of I: Purpose, possibility and responsibility](https://www.uidaho.edu/about/thinking-big/ai-at-uidaho)
- [Deep Background](https://checkplease.neocities.org/sift-toolbox/) - fact checking prompt from Mike Caulfield.
