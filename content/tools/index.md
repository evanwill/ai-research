While exploring research tools, keep in mind the parts of the research process and how AI and traditional tools might complement and support each other.

- Choosing a topic
- Finding sources
- Evaluating sources
- Citing sources

Considerations:

- Scope
    - What sources do they search or have access to? Discovery will be limited or biased based on what materials the tool includes in its index or training. 
    - Traditional databases can be limited to specific subscriptions or curated research sources.
    - AI platforms tend to be biased towards open access research and recent works.
- Reproducibility 
    - Traditional databases use indexes and boolean logic in a transparent way that can be repeated, ensuring approaches such as systematic review have verifiable coverage.
    - AI platforms are a black box with little transparency about how search is conducted. Some tools include links and citations to help provide more verification of sources.
- Cost
    - The Library spends millions of dollars to provide resources to the campus community. 
    - Individual subscriptions can be pricy.
- Privacy
    - AI (and increasingly academic publishers) collect significant data about users and their queries. 
    - Some research information is sensitive and can not be shared to third party companies.
