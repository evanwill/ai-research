Take five minutes to look at your browser tabs and reflect on the results of the showdown tests.

- How did your search string or AI prompt change?
- Which platforms seemed to produce the most relevant results?
- What features were most useful to get started on the research process?
- Compare your impressions of topics you knew a lot about vs nothing about.
- How did the conversational and authoritative tone of the AI outputs influence your search?
- What are the challenges you encountered in evaluating AI outputs vs traditional literature search?
- What are areas you would like to use (or avoid using) AI to facilitate the research process?
- What are the opportunities AI assistants open for your research?
- Do the AI assistants impact your ability to learn effectively or your research autonomy?

Each group will share one reflection they found interesting.
