Now take your search topic and craft a prompt for a general AI research tool to outline your question and find relevant sources in the field.
Choose a few AI tools to test your prompt and compare the results. 

Keep in mind your prompt--like any research question--is an iterative process. 
Research questions evolve with new information and scholarship rarely has definite answers.
Ask deeper questions to develop additional questions or lines of inquiry into your topic.

## Lateral Verification

Choose some of the citations generated by your AI summaries and verify them in our traditional databases. 

- Are the citations real?
- Does the content match the claims in the summary?
- Ask the model about its own blind spots (e.g., prompting the AI with, "What perspectives, methodologies, or data might be excluded from this summary?").
